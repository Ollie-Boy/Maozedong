Regenerate (square center-crop with slight upward bias for portraits):

  python3 scripts/generate_app_icon.py /path/to/毛泽东.jpg

  python3 scripts/generate_app_icon.py --url "http://www.cppcc.gov.cn/ViewFiels/2009/08/2009-08-26/ryzc/%E6%AF%9B%E6%B3%BD%E4%B8%9C.jpg"
