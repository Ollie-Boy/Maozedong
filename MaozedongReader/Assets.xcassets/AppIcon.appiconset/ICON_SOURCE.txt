App icon (1024×1024): red seal 「毛」 cropped from the calligraphy GIF:

  http://59.46.86.120:9002/shici/pic/shici/qinyuanchunxue.gif

Regenerate (after downloading the GIF locally):

  python3 scripts/generate_ma_seal_app_icon.py /path/to/qinyuanchunxue.gif

Or:

  python3 scripts/generate_ma_seal_app_icon.py --url "http://59.46.86.120:9002/shici/pic/shici/qinyuanchunxue.gif"

Note: The GIF is palette-based; the script maps palette indices to RGB (plain
Image.convert("RGB") can drop the red seal). Default crop box targets the large
bottom-left seal in this file.
